_The Erasmus+ Programme offers you the chance to spend from two to twelve months of your university career studying in another European country. As an Erasmus+ student, you can attend courses, sit exams and earn credits for your studies._ In some cases, internships are also possible.

See the [official information on the Erasmus+ Study programme](https://www.unibo.it/en/study/international-experiences/Studying-abroad/General-information-on-Erasmus/General-information-on-Erasmus).

For Erasmus+ Internship Mobility and Overseas, see the [Study Abroad Programs page]({{< relref "opportunities/in_bologna/study_abroad.md" >}}).
